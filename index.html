About GOLD MASTERS
GOLD MASTERS is a gold (XAUUSD) trading assistant for MetaTrader 5, together with a members' web dashboard. It reads the market, shows clear trade setups with Entry, Stop Loss and Take Profit levels, filters out risky news periods and keeps your account and membership in one place.


What you get


    

  • GOLD MASTERS EA for MT5. A chart panel that analyses gold and shows the market state, direction, confidence, Entry, Stop Loss and three Take Profit levels.
    • 

  • Trend and structure analysis. Fast and slow EMAs (20 / 50) for trend, plus recent support and resistance levels for stop placement.
    • 

  • Confidence filter. A setup is only confirmed when its confidence reaches at least 75% (default setting).
    • 

  • News protection. New signals are blocked from 30 minutes before to 15 minutes after high-impact USD news (default settings). If the news calendar is unavailable the EA stays on the safe side and blocks entries.
    • 

  • Manual and Auto modes. The EA starts in MANUAL mode: it shows signals and places no orders. AUTO mode must be switched on by you and needs a plan that allows it, a hedging account and a fully confirmed signal.
    • 

  • Built-in risk limits. Maximum total risk (default 3% of equity), maximum spread, maximum daily loss (default 3%) and a limit on open positions. After the third Take Profit, the remaining position can follow price with a trailing stop.
    • 

  • AI Gold Brief. A short market read in English or Amharic, built only from real price data, indicators, signals and news. It never promises profit.
    • 

  • Phone app. Works well on mobile and can be installed on your home screen like an app.
    • 

  • Members' dashboard. Economic news calendar with alerts before high-impact events, a signal centre, wallet and membership requests, and a protected EA download.
    • 

  • License protection. Your License Key works only with your own MT5 account number. The EA shows LICENSE ACTIVE or LOCKED.
    •

How to use GOLD MASTERS
Follow these steps in order. It takes about 10 minutes.


1. Prepare Telegram
  1. Open @goldmasters_otp_bot on Telegram and tap Start. Codes and alerts are sent there.
  2. Open @userinfobot, tap Start and copy the number it sends. This is your Telegram Chat ID.


2. Create your account
  1. Go to Login, then No account? Create one.
  2. Fill in your name, username, Telegram username, Telegram Chat ID, MT5 account number and a password. Email is optional.
  3. Type your MT5 account number carefully. Your license is tied to it. Find it at the top of your MT5 window.
  4. If a code is asked, enter the 6-digit code sent to your Telegram.
  5. Wait for the admin to approve your account.


3. Log in and download the EA
  1. After approval, log in. You will see your License Key and the Download EA button. Copy your License Key.
  2. Download the EA file (it ends in .ex5).


4. Install it in MetaTrader 5
  1. In MT5 choose File → Open Data Folder → MQL5 → Experts and paste the file there.
  2. Restart MT5, or right-click Expert Advisors in the Navigator and choose Refresh.
  3. Choose Tools → Options → Expert Advisors, tick Allow WebRequest for listed URL and add https://my-web-app-1-ge6h.onrender.com. Also tick Allow Algo Trading.
  4. Open an XAUUSD chart (your broker may call it XAUUSDm or GOLD) and drag the EA onto it.
  5. On the Inputs tab paste your License Key into LicenseKey and press OK.


5. Read the panel
  • LICENSE ACTIVE: everything works and signals are shown.
  • LOCKED: the license is not valid. See Troubleshooting below.
  • MANUAL mode (default): the EA shows signals only. You decide and place trades yourself.
  • AUTO MODE (button on the panel): the EA places trades by itself. It needs a Yearly or Lifetime plan, a hedging account, confidence of 75% or more and no high-impact news nearby. Test on a demo account first.
  • Trade levels: Entry, Stop Loss and Take Profit 1, 2 and 3. Keep the Stop Loss in place.


6. News alerts
Tap 🔔 News alerts in the menu and allow notifications. You will see a banner and get a pop-up before high-impact USD news (while the site is open). Avoid opening new trades around these times.


7. Updating the EA
When a new version is released, your account page shows Update available. Download the new file, remove the EA from the chart, replace the old file in MQL5/Experts, restart MT5 and attach the EA again with your License Key.


8. Use it like a phone app
Android (Chrome): open the site, tap 📲 Install app in the menu (or the browser menu → Install app). iPhone (Safari): tap Share and then Add to Home Screen. After that GOLD MASTERS opens full screen with its own icon. Use the bottom bar to move between Home, AI, Signals, News and Account.
9. AI Gold Brief
Open 🤖 AI after you log in with an approved account. You get the current trend, RSI, 24h range, upcoming high-impact news and a short written brief in English or አማርኛ. Tap Refresh for a new one (at most every 10 minutes). Treat it as information, not as a promise of profit.
10. Forgot your password?
Go to Login → Forgot password?, enter your username or Telegram ID, and enter the 6-digit code that arrives in your Telegram together with a new password. You must have pressed Start on @goldmasters_otp_bot.


Troubleshooting
  • LOCKED: check that the License Key is pasted exactly, that you use the same MT5 account number you registered, that your account is approved, and that your free trial or plan has not expired.
  • Error 4014 / WebRequest: add the website address in Tools → Options → Expert Advisors (step 4).
  • No signals: the EA waits for full confirmation, or news protection is active. This is normal.
  • Wrong MT5 number: ask support on Telegram to correct it.
  • Never share your License Key or the EA file with anyone.


Safety tips
  • Start on a demo account. Use a small lot size (default is 0.01).
  • Never risk money you cannot afford to lose. No signal is guaranteed.
  • Keep your computer or VPS online if you use AUTO mode.
